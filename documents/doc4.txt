Smart technology is being used to reduce the environmental impact of major sporting events like the World Cup.
For example, solar-powered stadiums and AI-driven energy management systems help cut carbon emissions.
Smartphone apps allow fans to track their carbon footprint and offset it through green projects.
Meanwhile, wearable devices monitor athlete health in real time, reducing medical waste.
As climate change threatens outdoor sports with extreme heat and flooding, tech companies are stepping up to help the sports industry adapt and survive.
